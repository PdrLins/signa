Before the App Store (launch blockers)

Privacy policy, terms and support page

Blocker

App Store Connect asks for a privacy policy URL and a support URL; Apple reviewers open them in a browser. The app then opens the same pages in an in-app browser. Drafts in English and Portuguese, written from what Signa really stores: data and retention, LGPD rights with the data protection contact (encarregado), PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25, "not investment advice", Yahoo / Apple / Telegram as third parties. A lawyer should review them.

A. Back-end serves the pagesGET /privacy, /terms on the API's address. No extra hosting; the link looks technical (api.signa.app/privacy).
B. Small static website (recommended)A website/ folder hosted free (Cloudflare Pages / GitHub Pages) on your domain. Also holds the landing page for ads and creators, the support page, Apple's universal-links file and, later, public stock pages for Google.

Needs: a domain for Signa (e.g. signa.app or signa.com.br); a contact email for privacy requests.
