Security

3 / 5

Strong

  • Every read, edit and delete is checked against the signed-in user (no cross-user access found).
  • Sign-in tokens: pinned algorithm, strong secret enforced, expiry, revocation, sessions per device, single-use iOS refresh tokens with reuse detection.
  • Sign-in codes: hashed, 3 attempts, 30 s expiry, constant-time checks. Passwords: bcrypt.
  • No injection paths; no slow-regex attacks (tested). Errors never leak database text.
  • Admin routes owner-only; "View as" only for the owner in development.

Weak

  • High Behind the web app's proxy, a client can fake its IP and skip every per-IP limit, or lock out someone else's IP. Safe when the phone calls the back-end directly.
  • High If the database ever reports a missing access column, every user becomes owner (confirmed in code). One-line fix: fail closed.
  • Med Unsigned-in public stock pages can be used to hammer Yahoo with random symbols; Yahoo then throttles Signa for everyone.
  • Med Anyone can keep a known account locked (1 wrong password every 15 min); usernames can be guessed.
  • Med No request size limit: a huge sign-up body is read into memory.
  • Med Two-step sign-in can be turned on without the password; a password reset doesn't sign out other devices.
  • Low "Resend code" can flood the shared Telegram bot and resets attempt counts.

Privacy and data protection

2 / 5

Strong

  • No user ever sees another user's data; password hashes never returned.
  • Codes and refresh tokens stored only as hashes; nightly cleanup of old tokens, sessions, logs.
  • "Followed together" stores no user ids; the growth report and feedback triage are owner-only.
  • Apple Search Ads tokens are cleared once resolved; Yahoo only ever sees ticker symbols.

Weak

  • High No account deletion route (the function exists, nothing calls it; iOS shows "not available yet"). Required by Apple and LGPD. Design proposed: 30-day grace, restore on sign-in, "delete now".
  • High After deletion, security logs and problem reports would keep IPs, device details and messages.
  • Med No data export (LGPD / PIPEDA right of access).
  • Med Logs record username + IP + every path (tickers, device tokens); a queued Telegram message can log a sign-in code.
  • Med Lock-screen pushes show money amounts (recap, dividends) with no "hide amounts" option.
  • Med "Followed together" can be broken with 4 fake accounts to learn a real user's other holdings.
  • Low Daily activity and sign-up sources are kept forever.

Abuse and fraud

2 / 5
  • Med With open sign-up there is no bot check: 5 fake friends give anyone +25 free stocks.
  • Med 5+ bots following AAPL and a chosen ticker make that ticker appear as "people who follow AAPL also follow X". That's a stock-promotion channel.
  • Med Bots also pollute the growth report you'll use to judge ads.
  • Fix: reward a referral only after real use (7 days, several active days, different device); count "followed together" only from accounts 30+ days old and active; raise the minimum to 10 while small; Turnstile on web, App Attest on iOS.

Performance

4 / 5

Strong

  • One Yahoo fetch per symbol for everyone, failures cached briefly, separate thread pools for jobs and requests.
  • Sign-in check answered from memory; per-user cache with safe invalidation; paged reads, chunked filters, gzip.
  • Chart math in milliseconds for 300 holdings.

Weak

  • High The nightly "followed together" count grows with the square of each user's list: 1,000 users × 200 stocks took 13 s and about 1 GB. Fix: drop symbols with fewer than 5 followers first, or count in Postgres.
  • Med Stock pages don't remember a Yahoo failure, so people waiting on the same symbol retry one by one.
  • Low "Similar" builds every candidate row before keeping 10; the hourly pool reload blocks the requests that need it.

Reliability and operations

2 / 5
  • High Crash: opening a new stock while someone else views "Similar" can raise "dictionary changed size", which returns a 500 (reproduced). Fix: never change the shared pool in place.
  • High Behind the web proxy, all web users share one IP bucket: the 6th sign-up in 15 min blocks web sign-in for everyone.
  • Med No error tracking: unhandled errors don't reach the log file, no request ids, no Sentry (the app already expects a Sentry id).
  • Med No alert when a job fails or is skipped; a Mac asleep at 16:30 or on the 1st loses that day's snapshot or the month's recap.
  • Med Nothing stops two copies running: jobs and pushes would run twice.
  • Med During a database outage all 32 request threads wait on timeouts (no circuit breaker), and Premium users briefly see the upgrade screen.
  • Med Not deployed: no host, no backups plan (Supabase Pro for daily backups), logs only on the Mac.

Data correctness

3 / 5
  • Med The monthly recap counts deposits as gains: adding R$10k shows "+R$10k" for the month.
  • Med If the currency lookup fails, snapshots are saved in CAD for everyone; users without settings default to CAD instead of their country's currency.
  • Med No snapshot on days only B3 trades (US/Canadian holidays).
  • Med The growth report mixes UTC and New York days at the edges.
  • Low No sanity band on non-CAD exchange rates; a bad Yahoo print would mis-size a currency for an hour.

Product

3 / 5

Strong

  • Works in any market; "PETR4" finds B3; pence handled; exchange holidays.
  • Easy entry: paste tickers, CSV import in Portuguese or English, decimal commas, undo import.
  • Push asked at the right moment; complete Portuguese (0 missing strings); Face ID, hide amounts, sessions list.
  • Plans can move features between Free and Premium with one database change.

Gaps that matter for keeping users

  • High Brazil: no fixed income (Tesouro, CDB, LCI) or CDI benchmark; Kinvo and Investidor10 have it.
  • High No broker import presets (B3 Área do Investidor, Wealthsimple, Questrade, Schwab); typing a portfolio is where people quit.
  • High Forgot password = lost account (no email or reset route; the code exists, unrouted).
  • Med Dividends received aren't logged automatically for people without transactions.
  • Med Free is very generous (up to 40 stocks with friends); Premium has little a Brazilian would pay for.

App Store readiness

1 / 5
  • Blocker In-app account deletion (stub today).
  • Blocker Privacy policy, terms and support URLs (the strings exist, no pages, nothing linked).
  • Blocker iOS privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) missing for the app and the widget.
  • Blocker "Upgrade to Premium" leads to "coming soon": hide it for v1, or ship In-App Purchase.
  • Blocker Production hosting, paid Apple account, push key, a demo account for App Review.
  • Med Claims to fix: "prices every minute" vs "delayed 15 min"; lock-screen widgets promised but not built; old "10 stocks" text on the web.
  • App Privacy label data is listed by the review (financial info, identifiers, usage, attribution; no tracking).

Monetization

1 / 5
  • Ready: Free/Premium gates on every feature, limits with upgrade prompts, pricing draft per market, creator plan.
  • Missing: In-App Purchase (StoreKit 2, Apple's purchase messages, Premium from the subscription, restore and manage buttons), final monthly and yearly prices, free trial, offer codes. This also blocks the creator program.

Growth measurement

4 / 5
  • Ready: sign-up sources, Apple Search Ads attribution, daily activity, funnel by channel, campaign, country or week.
  • Missing: crash and error rates, day-1/7/30 retention, push open rates, App Store rating prompt; hide groups smaller than 5 people.

Code quality and tests

4 / 5
  • Ready: 849 tests in about a minute, every route documented, speed rules written down, migrations folded into one schema.
  • Missing tests: Yahoo down on public pages, rate limits behind a proxy, day boundaries, recap with deposits, Premium during a database outage.
